Brand-aware packaging sorting · HKU campus

Which bin does this packaging go in?

HKU students and staff face a fixed set of recycling bins but packaging that is rarely obvious — a milk carton, a coffee cup, a noodle pot. This assistant names the packaging from a product or brand, matches it to a published HKU rule, and answers with the exact bin plus the official source. If it genuinely cannot tell, it asks — then commits to a labelled best guess rather than shrugging.

Who it is for, and what it is not

The problem, the users and the boundary of the system.

USERS

Anyone sorting on campus

Students and staff who know the item but not which of the small set of campus bins or the re Kiosk takes it.

PROBLEM

Rules exist, but are hard to apply

HKU publishes DO / DON'T guides and photos. Turning "this carton" into the right bin still needs domain knowledge.

NOT FOR

No guessing with hazmat

Chemicals, e-waste, medical waste and pressurised cans are refused, not sorted. It does not decide waste policy.

Scope change from proposal: the daily Smart Recycling Bin stations on campus carry four recycling bins (Paper, Metal, Plastic Bottle, Other Plastic) plus a general-waste bin. Tetra Pak, glass and rechargeable batteries are therefore guided to the re Kiosk, not to a bin that is not there. This replaced an earlier six-way re Kiosk framing.

The clarify branch: when the packaging is ambiguous, the app asks one question with fixed options. You answer; it may ask up to two rounds. On the third round it commits to a best_guess — labelled and confidence-capped — instead of refusing. Hazard items and out-of-scope requests bypass this and are refused outright.

Key design rule: the AI identifies; it never decides a category. Categories exist only in data/rules.json, and the matcher is the only component that emits one. If no rule matches, the answer is a refusal — a category is never invented.

Domain customisation

Limitations

Stated against the evidence above.

Fixed rule set

Only the 16 published rules are covered. Anything outside them is refused rather than guessed. Covered items must be clean and correctly described.

Text-first

Image upload, fuzzy matching and streaming responses are reserved interfaces, not shipped features. Input is normalised for English; Chinese queries are not guaranteed.

Location nuance

The official site lists a Glass Bottle slot at two Smart Recycling Bin locations. The app defaults to the re Kiosk, which accepts glass campus-wide — simpler, but not location-aware.

Model dependence

The AI path needs the course key and network. Without it the keyword path still answers covered items; everything else degrades visibly rather than guessing.

Rules come from HKU's official waste-reduction site. These are the authority for every category shown.
